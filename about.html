Meet Emily

Paws & Pals Pet Care was created by Emily Carter after seeing how difficult it can be for busy pet owners to find dependable care.

Emily believes pets do best when their normal routines are respected. Every visit is planned around the pet's personality, feeding schedule, exercise needs, and comfort.

Whether you need a quick walk, a daytime check-in, or overnight companionship, the goal is always the same: safe and thoughtful care.
